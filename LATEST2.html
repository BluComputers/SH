The Dundee Law

By Sarah Halligan

There's a hill in eastern Scotland
The locals call it the Dundee Law
Where the tourists climb to see the view
And remember those who went before

Atop there is a monument
To those who in battle fell
But this place holds her memories
She guards her secrets well

The Law remembers the Christmas storm
Of eighteen seventy nine
And she remembers that distant whistle
As the train came down the line

That night the storm was wild and free
No man should have been so bold
The Tay flowed fast, her waves were high
Her waters dark and cold

The wind it blew a hurricane.
And the bridge it bowed and broke
And into the Tay all lives were lost
Amongst the lightning and the smoke

On the 28th of December
Climb the Law and listen well
In the darkness a distant whistle
This is the night she fell

There's a hill in eastern Scotland
The locals call it the Dundee Law
Where the tourists climb to see the view
And remember those who went before

Magnus and the Gruffalo,s Godzilla adventure!!

By Sarah Halligan

So today i was tasked with writing something for a very special little boy called Magnus over in Edinburgh. Now Magnus is 5 years old and he loves the Gruffalo and dinosaurs. He really really loves Godzilla! He also has a pet gecko called Geccy ! Oh and Magnus had to be in the poem aswell. So I asked his dad to ask him if he could give Godzilla a present what would he give? And Magnus said a dinosaur tooth! So with a huge thank you to Julia Donaldson for the inspiration and my own childhood memories I give you Magnus and the Gruffalo,s Godzilla adventure!!

The Gruffalo said to the Gruffalo,s child
And held it up to the world as proof
"As I was walking through the deep dark wood
I found a dinosaur tooth!

Now I know Godzilla collects such things
So I'm really happy to give
But how on earth will I find him?
Does anyone know where he lives?"

The Gruffalo,s child smiled and said
"You need to go to Scotland dad
Magnus in Edinburgh will show you the way
He's a very special little lad "

Now up there in Scotland all was quiet
Mum and dad were gently creeping
Out of the room on tiptoes
Magnus was finally sleeping

Then deep in the night it happened
The sound of the wind around the room
Magnus ran to the window
To see a truly magnificent broom!

And there on the grass was the Gruffalo!
With a wave and a beaming smile
"Hello there Magnus nice to meet you
Can I borrow you for a while?

I've heard you know Godzilla
He's scary and fills me with dread
But I have a dinosaur tooth to give him
Perhaps you'd like to give it instead?

I've borrowed this broom from a witch friend of mine
It's faster than Santa's sleigh
And I've packed some scrambled snake sandwiches
We can eat them along the way!"

"What about Geccy and my dinosaurs?
They'll get lonely if I'm not there"
"Oh bring them along!" The Gruffalo said
"We have plenty of room to spare!

There's seats for us and seats for them
Come on everyone clamber on!"
The Gruffalo tapped the broomstick
3,2,1 and whoosh they were gone!

Over the buildings and over the trees
Loop the loop and twice round the moon
In the distance Magnus could see the ocean
Gruffalo said "We'll be there soon!"

The air grew warmer and Magnus knew
They'd reached Godzilla,s lair
He tapped the Gruffalo on the shoulder
"I think he's somewhere down there!"

Then the water started to bubble
And smoke began to rise
Godzilla leapt from under the water
Magnus couldn't believe his eyes!

"Next time he jumps throw him the tooth!"
Gruffalo held the broomstick steady
"Here he comes Magnus I see him
Give it your best shot, are you ready?"

With an almighty leap Godzilla rose up
Magnus could see right into his jaws
"Here Godzilla this is for you!"
As the tooth landed right in his claws

Down went Godzilla with an enormous splash
Then turned one last time in the deep
Then rose up again higher than ever
In one final thank you leap!

The water now calm they said their goodbyes
"Great job!" The Gruffalo said
"Come on my friend its nearly morning
Let's get you back to bed!"

Magnus woke up as dad came in
"Morning Magnus how was your night?
Any good dreams to tell me about?
Any amazing sights?"

Magnus smiled to himself as he remembered the night
No way was he going to snitch
And for breakfast that morning Magnus ate, can you guess?
Hahaha yes! The last remaining scrambled snake sandwich!
