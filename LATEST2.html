Knackered, old and grey!

By Sarah Halligan

Will all of you still love me
When I'm knackered, old and grey?
Will anyone still listen
To the things I have to say?

Will anyone read my poems?
Will my fingers still want to type?
Will growing old disgracefully
Live up to all the hype?

The end of the cheeky messages
Nobody asking for my pics
I'll need me readers on for sure
To see all their wrinkly dicks!

Will there even be a Facebook?
Will anybody care?
Will I need a Stannah stairlift
To get me up the stairs?

I'll sit here in my rocking chair
Full of memories and tears
And go back across the miles and smiles
Through all my yester years

I have to say I had a blast
No complaints, it's been a treat!
I played the cards that life has dealt
From the bitter to the sweet

So will all of you still love me?
Will you still hold me in your embrace?
I'll be knackered, I'll be old and grey
And Bo and Obs will be round my waist!
