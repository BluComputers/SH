Thank you

Thank you

By Sarah Halligan

Today we've said a goodbye to my auntie Sandra. Mum's big sister. The two most influential women in my family are gone. Reunited in heaven. Sandra was an absolute rock for me and dad after mum died . She was there for us despite her own grief and her own illness. All i can say to her, to both of them is thank you.

Thank you for the cuddles
Thank you for the kiss
Thank you for the memories
Thank you for the bliss

Thank you for the touches
Thank you for advice
Thank you, I know I needed to
Thank you once or twice

Thank you for the teaching
Thank you for being there
Thank you for keeping me steady
Thank you for healing my despair

Thank you for what you did for me
Thank you from me and dad
Thank you for trying to fix it
Thank you for the happy when I was sad

Thank you from my broken heart
Thank you for a love so true
Thank you for being a part of me
Thank you for being you 💔💔
