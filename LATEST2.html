Memories and Macca

By Sarah Halligan

I have the fondest memories
Of waterfalls and rain
Those days in the heart of the country
Now replaced by days of pain

As I rise to face another day
Mum's photo smiles so bright
Her beautiful face is the first I see
I still kiss her every night

I know if she was here today
The sun would always shine
They would dance to silly love songs
Sweet memories of a time

Mum, I know that you are with us
Your loving flame shines clear and bright
Please help dad win his tug of war
Between the darkness and the light

And maybe with a little luck
He'll find sweet love again
And there will be no more lonely nights
No more lonliness and pain

When I See My Daddy Smile

By Sarah Halligan

When I see my daddy smiling
I feel so very proud
Together we've walked so many miles
Weighed down by grief's dark cloud

There was a time not long ago
When his world was torn apart
Nothing I could say or do
Would mend his broken heart

But every day I tried so hard
Did my best to pull him through
To make him realise there's still a point
Now that we are two

We make the best of what we have
Our daddy daughter team
We still have our happy memories
Despite all the might have beens

Life is getting better now
Together we'll find the key
To the happiness I feel the most
When my daddy smiles at me ❤️❤️

The Dundee Law

By Sarah Halligan

There's a hill in eastern Scotland
The locals call it the Dundee Law
Where the tourists climb to see the view
And remember those who went before

Atop there is a monument
To those who in battle fell
But this place holds her memories
She guards her secrets well

The Law remembers the Christmas storm
Of eighteen seventy nine
And she remembers that distant whistle
As the train came down the line

That night the storm was wild and free
No man should have been so bold
The Tay flowed fast, her waves were high
Her waters dark and cold

The wind it blew a hurricane.
And the bridge it bowed and broke
And into the Tay all lives were lost
Amongst the lightning and the smoke

On the 28th of December
Climb the Law and listen well
In the darkness a distant whistle
This is the night she fell

There's a hill in eastern Scotland
The locals call it the Dundee Law
Where the tourists climb to see the view
And remember those who went before

I'm Leaving Home!

By Sarah Halligan

I'm running away from home today
Daddy shouted at me
How was I supposed to know
Crayons aren't allowed on the settee?

I'm running away from home today
Mummy's wearing an awful frown
I trampled mud into the carpet again
Why don't they buy one that's brown?

I'm running away from home today
My big sister told on me
All because I lifted her skirt up outside school
Well i thought it was funny heehee!

I'm running away from home today
Now even the dog is being rude!
Every time I go near him he runs away
Doesn't he like pickled onions in his food?

I'm running away from home today
Nobody around here wants me
On second thoughts maybe I'll leave tomorrow
We're going to Maccies for tea!

Taboo

By Sarah Halligan

She hasn't done her homework
She knows he'll make her stay
She yearns for her weekly punishment
Oh how she loves detention day

Her friends think she's a rebel
But this secret no one can know
A forbidden love like this one
Can never be on show

She rises early to shower
A touch of his favourite scent
The white lace beneath her uniform
Gives him her consent

Their eyes meet across the classroom
In that glance a burning fire
Now they must wait for the final bell
Before releasing their desire

Because she hasn't done her homework
And now he tells her she must stay
The punishment she longs for
Oh how she loves detention day
