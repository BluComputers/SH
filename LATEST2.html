Memories and Macca

By Sarah Halligan

I have the fondest memories
Of waterfalls and rain
Those days in the heart of the country
Now replaced by days of pain

As I rise to face another day
Mum's photo smiles so bright
Her beautiful face is the first I see
I still kiss her every night

I know if she was here today
The sun would always shine
They would dance to silly love songs
Sweet memories of a time

Mum, I know that you are with us
Your loving flame shines clear and bright
Please help dad win his tug of war
Between the darkness and the light

And maybe with a little luck
He'll find sweet love again
And there will be no more lonely nights
No more lonliness and pain
